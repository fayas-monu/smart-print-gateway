Terms & Conditions

AL SHAJAR STATIONERY · Smart Print Gateway

1. Who we are

This portal is operated by AL SHAJAR STATIONERY (SHS) in the United Arab Emirates. By sending a print job you agree to these terms.

2. Prices and VAT

All prices are in UAE Dirhams (AED) and already include 5% UAE VAT. VAT is never added on top of the amount shown. The receipt reverses the VAT for transparency.

  • A4 and A5 black & white: AED 1.00 per 8 pages, or part of 8, single- or double-sided. 9–16 pages cost AED 2.00.
  • A4 and A5 colour: AED 1.00 per page.
  • A3 black & white: AED 2.00 per page; A3 colour: AED 3.00 per page.
  • Copies multiply the number of pages. Duplex and N-up change paper use, not the per-page price.

3. Delivery

Home delivery is available within the UAE. Print orders of AED 50.00 or more qualify for free delivery; below that a delivery fee of AED 10.00 applies. The current fee and threshold are always shown in the order summary before you send the job. You must provide a correct address and phone number.

4. Payment

No payment is taken online. Choose cash or card and pay at the counter on collection, or to the driver on delivery.

5. Job review and refusal

Our operators review every job before printing. We may reject a job if a file is unreadable, password-protected, contains unlawful or prohibited content, or infringes copyright. If a job is rejected, you will not be charged for it.

6. Your responsibility

Please check the live preview, page range, paper size, ink and copies carefully. Colours on screen may differ slightly from printed colours, and printer margins may trim very close edges. Once a job has been printed, we cannot refund customer errors such as the wrong file, page range or layout.

7. Copyright

You confirm that you own or have permission to reproduce every file you upload. You are responsible for any third-party rights in the content.

8. File storage

Uploaded files are stored on the shop computer only. Completed, rejected and cancelled jobs are automatically deleted after 30 days, unless a different retention period is configured by the shop.

9. Liability

Our liability for any job is limited to the amount paid for that job. We are not liable for indirect losses.

10. Contact

Please speak to the counter team for any question about these terms.

Privacy Policy

How your files and details are handled

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number, and — for delivery — your address.
  • Optional notes for the print desk.
  • The files you upload and the print options you choose.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the local service, such as your device address on the shop network.

Why we collect it

Only to review, print and hand over your job, and to deliver it when you choose home delivery. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decisions.

Where it is stored

Your files and details are stored on the shop’s own computer, not in a cloud service. This portal does not load third-party analytics, tracking pixels or advertising scripts. Fonts, PDF previews and QR codes are served locally.

Who can see it

Only the AL SHAJAR STATIONERY print staff who operate the PIN-protected console. If you request delivery, the driver receives the address and phone number needed to complete the delivery.

How long we keep it

Completed, rejected and cancelled jobs are automatically deleted after 30 days. Active jobs remain until they are finished. You can ask the counter to delete a job earlier.

Security

The operator console is protected by a PIN and signed, HTTP-only session cookies. The service is designed to run on the shop’s local network. We still recommend that you do not upload sensitive documents you would not normally hand to a print shop.

Your choices

You can ask staff to view, correct or delete your job details and files. You can also choose store pickup to avoid providing a delivery address.

Contact

Please speak to the counter team for any privacy request.
